3. ITEM 2: STAY-DATE SEMANTICS & CHECKOUT-DAY OPERATIONAL ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Date Validation Matrix:
   +-----------------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------+---------------------------+
   | Scenario                    | Evaluated Expression  | System Behavior             | Verification Test         |
   +-----------------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------+---------------------------+
   | Order date in past          | orderDate < today     | PAST_DATE_NOT_ALLOWED (400) | testRequireEligible_Past  |
   | Null order date             | orderDate == null     | PAST_DATE_NOT_ALLOWED (400) | testRequireEligible_Null  |
   | Date before check-in        | orderDate < checkIn   | DATE_OUTSIDE_STAY (400)     | testRequireEligible_Out   |
   | Exact check-in day          | orderDate == checkIn  | ACCEPTED (Valid Order)      | testExactBoundaries_Acc   |
   | Intermediate stay date      | checkIn < d < checkOut| ACCEPTED (Valid Order)      | testRequireEligible_Valid |
   | Exact check-out day         | orderDate == checkOut | ACCEPTED (Valid Order)      | testExactBoundaries_Acc   |
   | Date after check-out        | orderDate > checkOut  | DATE_OUTSIDE_STAY (400)     | testRequireEligible_Out   |
   | Inverted stay dates         | checkIn > checkOut    | INVALID_BOOKING_DATES (400) | testInconsistentStayDates |
   | Cancelled retreat booking   | status == 'CANCELLED' | BOOKING_NOT_ACTIVE (400)    | testCancelledBooking_Rej  |
   +-----------------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------+---------------------------+

B. Explicit Operational Flag: Checkout-Day Dining Semantics:
   - Current Code Rule: Date-level comparison (`orderDate.isAfter(checkOut)`). Orders on `checkOutDate`
     are ACCEPTED for any meal period (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or A-la-carte).
   - Operational Analysis:
     * In standard resort operations, check-out occurs at 11:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
     * While breakfast or checkout-morning room service is standard, placing a dinner order
       (e.g., 19:00) on the checkout date after the guest has settled their folio and departed
       is potentially inconsistent with resort physical residency.
     * Furthermore, if the folio is settled at 12:00 PM, subsequent F&B charges would be blocked
       by the closed/settled folio guard, or would result in uncollected revenue if delivered post-checkout.
   - Status: UNDOCUMENTED RESORT OPERATIONAL POLICY (Finding Severity: P3).
     * Recommendation: The current date-level check is technically safe for prototype testing.
       For production, the resort operations committee must document whether checkout-day orders
       are restricted to breakfast/lunch or cut off at checkout time (e.g., 12:00 PM).

     Order status remains in `READY` or `PREPARING`.

   - `DELIVERED` is terminal: `if ("DELIVERED".equals(currentStatus)) throw ORDER_ALREADY_DELIVERED;`
   - `CANCELLED` is terminal: `if ("CANCELLED".equals(currentStatus)) throw ORDER_CANCELLED;`

   - Verified by test: `FnbBillingIntegrationTest` lines 173-205.

5. ITEM 4: CANCELLATION AUDIT & AUTHORIZATION AUDIT

A. State & Role Permissions for Cancellation:
   - Customer Role (`isStaff = false`):
     * Allowed ONLY when order is in `PENDING` state.
     * Attempting cancellation in `PREPARING` or `READY` throws `CUSTOMER_CANNOT_CANCEL_PREPARING_ORDER`.
     * Attempting cancellation on another user's booking throws `AccessDeniedException`.
   - Staff Role (`isStaff = true`):
     * Permitted from `PREPARING` and `READY`.
     * Mandatory Reason: `if (trimmedReason == null || trimmedReason.isEmpty()) throw CANCELLATION_REASON_REQUIRED;`

   - Audit action: `CANCEL_MEAL_ORDER`
   - Target type: `MealOrder`
   - Target ref: String representation of `meal_order_id`
   - Actor: `effectiveUserId`
   - Role: `CUSTOMER` or resolved staff role (`FNB_STAFF`, `MANAGER`, `ADMIN`)
   - Old value: previous status (`PENDING`, `PREPARING`, `READY`)
   - New value: `"CANCELLED; Reason: " + trimmedReason`
   - Audit Failure Semantics: `auditLogService.record(...)` is executed before persisting the entity
     mutation. An audit storage failure aborts the cancellation transaction without mutating the order.
     Verified by test: `MealOrderServiceTest.testCancel_AuditFailure_ConsistentWithTransactionContract`.
